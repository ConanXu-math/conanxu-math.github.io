## Hi, I'm Conan Xu

I work on **AI4Math, optimization, and formal mathematics**, and build tools that support research and learning.

My public projects explore the convergence and failure of ADMM iterations, the formalization of optimization algorithms in Lean, and ways to use AI in mathematical research and textbook study.

### Research and practice

- **Mathematics and optimization**: understanding why algorithms converge, when they fail, and the mathematics behind numerical behavior.
- **Formal mathematics**: using Lean 4 to make the connections between assumptions, algorithms, and proofs explicit.
- **AI and research tools**: exploring practical ways to support research, textbook reading, and the organization of learning materials.

The [projects page](/projects/) provides links to the code and documentation.

### About this blog

This is a place for research notes, project write-ups, and things I learn along the way. Chinese and English articles are maintained separately; use **中文 / English** at the top of the page to switch languages.

The blog includes introductions based on public project materials, along with derivations and ideas developed while studying. Project introductions retain links to their original repositories or papers for further reading.
